01 / Research

Quantum measurements.
Complex optical systems.

My research combines quantum optics, precision interferometry, and physically interpretable modeling to study how quantum fluctuations and correlations shape the information available from a measurement.

Quantum measurement & back-action

I use Advanced LIGO to investigate measurement imprecision, radiation-pressure back-action, and the correlations between them. My work combines squeezed-light enhancement, quantum reservoir engineering, and advanced optical readout to understand and control quantum measurements of macroscopic motion.

Nonlinear & multimode photonics

My doctoral research investigated soliton self-mode conversion, coherence, and energetic ultrafast frequency generation in multimode fibers. I have also worked on structured light and spatial combs, exploring how control of spatial and temporal modes can expand the capabilities of optical systems.

Looking ahead

My broader goal is to understand and engineer quantum information flow in complex physical systems through the joint design of input states, interactions, and measurements.
